Services

Flexible engagement models so we can pick the one that fits your project and budget:

Turnkey Project

A project with a limited, well-defined scope, delivered for a single fixed price — ideal for specific needs and quick deliveries.

Fixed Price per Milestone

The project is split into milestones, each with its own scope and price, giving you clarity and predictable costs.

Time & Material

Billing based on hours actually worked — total flexibility for evolving scope and continuous improvement.

Hybrid (Fixed MVP + T&M)

A fixed price to build the initial MVP, then hourly billing for adjustments, improvements and new features after approval.

Monthly Retainer

A monthly package of dedicated hours for maintenance, support and small improvements, with priority response.
