About

SrNando is the independent studio of Fernando Gonçalves, a full-stack developer based in Lisbon, Portugal. I build web applications and SaaS products end to end — front end, back end, database, payments and integrations.

I work mainly with React and Supabase, and I enjoy the parts of a product that actually make it work: secure authentication, payment flows, third-party integrations and automation.

How I work

Clear scope first. Before writing code, we agree on what the project includes, a timeline and a price — no surprises.

Honest communication. You always know where the project stands. I reply quickly and in plain language (English or Portuguese).

Real, working software. I deliver products that are deployed and run in production, not just demos.
